01 / RIGHT NOW

Who are you???

Well, that's a good question. Honestly, I'm not sure I've completely defined that yet. I'm a 48-year-old wife and mother of four amazing, amazing kids. I love dogs, the warmth of the sun against my skin, the salty wind off the ocean, and laughter. I feel intensely. I daydream. I write short stories and poetry. I love writting. I am a writer.
I'm too loud without even realizing it. I talk too fast, and I get bored when others talk too slowly. I love everything that glows, and I like glitter… a lot. I love to read, especially fairy tales and fantasy. I am too mischeivous sometimes, people say, "You should be serious." I'm so sorry I think, I cant help it if you dont have a personality. Take happiness whe you can get it... becouse this world ... it provides too much saddness and disaapointments.

I love solving problems and puzzles. I get bored easily. I want to feel strong, happy, and valued. I want to be perfect, but I'm not. Attention to detail is almost an obsession. Failure hits hard. Possibilities are exciting, especially when they involve learning skills that can be stacked, built upon, and turned into something more. It feels like magic.

I usually joke and tell people, “I haven't decided what I want to be when I grow up yet.”

I have ADHD. I was diagnosed two years ago, and suddenly, so much of my life made sense. I finished college at 43, earning my bachelor's degree. At 45, I finally found the courage to pursue something I had longed to do. I joined a cohort at Code:You and began learning front-end development.

I felt alive. The endless problems were exhilarating. The different ways to reach the same solution were fascinating. And learning to solve problems with variables that once felt like the enemy? Empowering. There is something extraordinary about realizing that the things you once struggled with can become the very tools you use to build something new. That there isn't always one right answer. That you can experiment, break things, try again, and discover possibilities you hadn't even imagined.

Maybe I haven't decided what I want to be when I grow up because I'm still discovering all the things I can become.
And honestly? I think that's a pretty wonderful place to be.
